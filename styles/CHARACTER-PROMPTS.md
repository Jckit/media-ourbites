## stickman

Create one landscape 16:9 character concept sheet for OURBITES, a business explainer animation channel. White/off-white background, dark navy linework, teal accents, a little muted orange for financial props. Header text exactly "OURBITES". Small subtitle "Character concept: stickman". A true minimalist stick figure: circular cream head outlined in navy, two dot eyes, single expressive eyebrow, line body and thin line limbs, tiny teal necktie as its identifying feature. No hair, no filled torso or clothes except tie. Expressive poses achieved with just a few strokes.
