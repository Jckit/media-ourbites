# Retention evidence and adaptation

Researched 2026-10-27. These inform editorial hypotheses; none proves an Ourbites retention improvement.

## YouTube primary guidance

[Key moments for audience retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415): opening expectations should match title/thumbnail; consider moving compelling later moments earlier. Dips may indicate skipping or abandonment; spikes may indicate replay/sharing OR unclear explanation. Compare videos of similar length and audience segments. Data generally takes 1–2 days; some highlighted moments require 60 seconds and 100 views. Do not apply the 30-second intro metric to a 17-second pilot as if it were measured.

[Shorts view definitions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070): starts/replays and engaged views differ. Use engaged views to compare Shorts; do not treat raw starts as evidence that the hook held attention.

## MrBeast / Colin and Samir

[Original interview and publisher show notes](https://www.colinandsamir.com/resources/an-unfiltered-conversation-with-mrbeast): relevant chapters listed by the publisher include improving on YouTube (19:22), storytelling (27:50), copying the MrBeast style (38:15), and short form (48:22). Publisher notes establish themes, not a verbatim transcript or independently measured effects. Do not claim the full interview was watched.

[Third-party transcript of a Colin and Samir interview](https://singjupost.com/colin-and-samir-show-with-mrbeast-transcript/), section “Retention Strategy: Introducing Side Stories”: describes sustaining longer videos with additional storylines. Treat transcription fidelity as unverified and do not assume it is the same interview as the publisher page above. Our adaptation: every beat advances a question; a short business explanation does not need multiple distracting subplots.

## Ourbites recommendations, not creator quotations

Transfer clarity, character stakes, fulfilled promises and deliberate iteration. Do not copy spectacle budgets, shouting, arbitrary cuts-per-minute or universal retention targets. Preserve moments needed to understand accounting. Existing `research/COMPETITOR-FORMATS.md` supplies format hypotheses, not access to competitors' private analytics. Prioritize observed viewer friction over increasing a self-assigned score.
