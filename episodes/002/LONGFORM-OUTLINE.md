## One-sentence promise

**How Anthropic went from a research-focused AI startup to a multibillion-dollar enterprise platform, why its accounts show an extraordinary net loss, and what its proposed IPO actually asks investors to believe.**

Don't turn the long-form into six disconnected facts. Follow the money and the tradeoffs: product distribution drives revenue, frontier model delivery costs compute, financing allows scaling but leaves risks, and unusual governance affects the investment.
