# Ourbites recurring protagonist

The channel's **fictional visual guide** must be present in every Ourbites video. The character is distinct from actual company executives, the voice actor, and any generic extras.

**Provisional design:** the existing `hybrid` concept in [styles/CHARACTER-PROMPTS.md](../../styles/CHARACTER-PROMPTS.md). This character already appears as `HybridHost` in the ownership proof; the shared SVG in [src/OurbitesHost.tsx](../../src/OurbitesHost.tsx) makes it reusable instead of copying shapes into separate video projects.

**Personality:** intellectually curious, a little skeptical of overly tidy business headlines, conversational and dry rather than hyperactive. The character does not perform fake corporate interviews or speak on behalf of a real executive.

**Supported prototype poses:** `neutral`, `explain`, `think`, `point`, `react`. A single base silhouette, face and palette must survive each pose. The character can appear in hooks, financial explanations, question transitions or payoffs; avoid covering sources, diagrams and caption areas.

**Design gate:** the host's final official look is **awaiting Justin's approval**. Existing SVG is a working prototype, not a final signed-off brand asset. Use `profile.json` as the machine-readable contract, `references.md` as the source record, and `research/CHARACTER-ASSETS.md` as the SOP. After approval record revision, file/hash or commit, date, reviewer and any changed pose list.
